Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

ClipPoster ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal tool that uploads short video clips to the TikTok account of the person using it. This policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information we access

When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, TikTok gives the App, with your permission:

  • Basic profile information (such as your open ID, display name and avatar), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • An access token that lets the App upload videos to your account with the permissions you approved (video.upload and, if granted, video.publish).

The App does not access your messages, followers, contacts, viewing history, location, or payment information.

2. How we use information

  • To upload videos you choose to your TikTok account, either as drafts or as posts.
  • To show the status of those uploads.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the App.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens and upload logs are stored locally on the computer where the App runs. They are not sent to any server operated by us. Video files are sent only to TikTok through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Information is exchanged only with TikTok as required to upload your videos.

5. Data retention and deletion

You can disconnect the App at any time by removing it in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services (or similar). Deleting the App's local files removes all stored tokens and logs. You can also contact us to request deletion of any information related to you.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your country) and does not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.

8. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on GitHub
